# Verification report

Generated by `python -m mutashabihat.verify`. Every number below is computed, not typed.

## What "accurate" means here

Every link is re-checked against the Quran text (QUL's QPC Hafs script). A pair is kept only if its two
ayahs share real wording, or if a human-curated source lists it (then it is labelled `low` or `unverified`).
This proves the wording claim. It is not a scholarly review: nothing here replaces a hafiz or a classical
mutashabihat reference, and `unverified` pairs in particular need a human look.

## 3. How specific is the text test?

100000 random ayah pairs (seed 2026) were run through the same classifier:

| Evidence level | Random pairs | Rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| strong | 80 | 0.08% |
| moderate | 168 | 0.17% |
| weak | 99752 | 99.75% |

Unrelated ayahs almost never pass, so a link with strong evidence did not arise by chance.

Why a 2-word overlap is not evidence: how often random pairs share a fuzzy run of at least k words:

| k | Random pairs | Rate |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 2037 | 2.04% |
| 3 | 232 | 0.23% |
| 4 | 70 | 0.07% |
| 5 | 23 | 0.02% |

Two shared words occur by chance far more often than three, so a pair backed only by a 2-word overlap is rejected unless a human source lists it or a second source agrees. Some of these are genuine short mutashabihat (see SPOT_CHECK.md); `data/rejected.json` is the pool to review for them.

## 6. QUL phrase layer

814 phrases, 3558 occurrences. Each occurrence was re-anchored in the text: {'identical': 2159, 'variant': 1196, 'unrelated': 192, 'unanchored': 11}. 8 occurrences (0.22%) had QUL word indexes off by a word or more and were moved. `identical` = same words as the source phrase, `variant` = similar (QUL phrases are clusters of similar wordings), `unrelated` = no real match; treat `unrelated` as errors.

## 7. Known limits

- QUL's data license is unconfirmed; the other sources' terms are in `sources/MANIFEST.json` and the README.
- The QUL phrase file is an Aug 2024 export covering 36% of ayahs; the live QUL site has more.
- Pairs that share only a formula phrase (e.g. جنات تجري من تحتها الأنهار خالدين فيها) are not listed unless a source lists them.
- Weak-evidence pairs from human sources may be thematic rather than verbal; they are kept as `unverified`/`low`, not dropped.
- srmdn's notes are third-party commentary and are kept apart in `data/notes_srmdn.json`.
